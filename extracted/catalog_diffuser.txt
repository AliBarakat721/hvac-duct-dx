CIRCULAR CEILING DIFFUSER & JET DIFFUSER:-
Circular ceiling diffuser and jet diffuser are always considered as a heavy duty air distribution
devices having a wide range of applications in heating , air conditioning and ventilation
systems. Circular ceiling diffuser provide an unusually uniform horizontal airflow patterns,
while jet diffuser provide an efficient spot projection for air projection for air direction and
orientation.

With uniformly assembled oncentric rings, this does not only produce optimum performance,
but also assure uniformity of appearance when different sizes are used in the same area.
Circular ceiling diffuser and jet diffuser are always suitable for most of the HVAC applications,
but they are especially well suited to factories , warehouses, convention halls, coliseums,
shopping malls and other applications where ceiling are high and conditions are variable.

TYPE CCD:
This type is suitable for flush installation in ceiling; they can be used for supply or extract
air.the design consists of a conical core followed by continuously concentric rings to adjust the
air flow pattern. This design feature will assure little turbulance in the air stream and allow for
the distribution of the smudge pattern . it also provides excellent air distribution efficiency.
MATERIAL &FINISH:-
The diffuser is fabricated from aluminum. Diffusers are available in white polyester powder
coating finish or any other RAL standard colour upon customer request.

 Pt.        : total pressure in inches of water. Equals the sum of static pressure plus velocity

          at a given terminal velocity . the throw is based on maximum terminal velocity of
          50 fpm and minimum terminal velocity of 100 fpm.
NC :      Noise Criteria are based on sound power level . to determine NC values in the room
         due to diffuser , subtract the room attenuation from the NC rating which are gives in
         the performance tables.
